Day 1 – Chest + Biceps

  • Barbell Bench Press – 4x6–8
    Heavy weight, progressive overload weekly
  • Incline Dumbbell Press – 4x8–10
    Controlled movement, focus on stretch
  • Cable Crossover (High to Low) – 3x12–15
    Full squeeze at the end
  • Machine Chest Press – 3x10–12
    To muscle failure
  • Standing Barbell Curl – 4x8–10
    Controlled weight
  • Alternating Dumbbell Curl – 3x10–12
    Supinate wrist at the top
  • Single-Handle Cable Curl – 3x12–15
    Focus on muscle contraction

Day 2 – Legs + Glutes + Calves

  • Barbell Squat – 4x6–8
    Full depth, heavy weight
  • Walking Dumbbell Lunge – 3x10–12/leg
    Controlled steps
  • Barbell Hip Thrust – 4x8–10
    2-second pause at the top
  • Leg Press (Feet Slightly Wider) – 3x10–12
    For more glute activation
  • Lying Leg Curl – 3x12–15
    Slow and controlled
  • Standing Calf Raise – 4x15–20
    Pause at the top
  • Seated Calf Raise – 3x20–25
    Full stretch at the bottom

Day 3 – Back + Triceps

  • Pull-Up or Lat Pulldown – 4x6–10
    To failure if possible
  • Barbell Row – 4x8–10
    Keep back straight
  • Single-Arm Dumbbell Row – 3x10–12
    Focus on stretch
  • Seated Cable Row – 3x12–15
    Full control
  • Straight Bar Triceps Pushdown – 4x8–10
    Avoid locking elbows
  • Lying Barbell Triceps Extension (Skull Crusher) – 3x10–12
    Elbows fixed
  • Bench Dips (Weighted if possible) – 3x12–15
    To failure

Day 4 – Shoulders + Abs

  • Seated Barbell Overhead Press – 4x6–8
    Heavy weight
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raise – 4x12–15
    No swinging
  • Front Raise (Dumbbell or Plate) – 3x10–12
    Controlled lift
  • Barbell or Dumbbell Shrug – 4x10–12
    Pause at the top
  • Cable Crunch – 3x15–20
    Full abdominal contraction
  • Plank – 3x45–60s
    Core tight
  • Hanging Leg Raise – 3x12–15
    Controlled movement
